## Chinese Astrology Compatibility: Combining Bazi and Zodiac Systems

Chinese astrology offers two powerful compatibility systems that work at different levels of depth:

1. **Chinese Zodiac compatibility** — based on your birth year (one layer)
2. **Bazi (Four Pillars of Destiny) compatibility** — based on your complete birth data (eight layers)

When used together, they provide one of the most comprehensive compatibility frameworks in any astrological tradition.

## Layer 1: Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Your Chinese zodiac sign (determined by your birth year) provides a quick but useful compatibility overview. The 12 signs form specific harmony and clash pairs.

### The 6 Harmonious Combinations (六合)

| Pair | Why It Works |
|------|-------------|
| Rat + Ox | Intelligence meets reliability |
| Tiger + Pig | Boldness meets acceptance |
| Rabbit + Dog | Gentleness meets loyalty |
| Dragon + Rooster | Vision meets precision |
| Snake + Monkey | Depth meets cleverness |
| Horse + Goat | Energy meets warmth |

### The 6 Major Clashes (六冲)

| Clash | Nature of Tension |
|-------|-------------------|
| Rat + Horse | Emotional depth vs. independence |
| Tiger + Monkey | Freedom vs. control |
| Rabbit + Rooster | Harmony-seeking vs. criticism |
| Dragon + Dog | Ambition vs. caution |
| Snake + Pig | Introspection vs. spontaneity |
| Ox + Goat | Stability vs. change |

### The 3 Harmonious Triangles

Beyond the 6 pairs, signs also form harmonious **triangles** — groups of three signs that naturally support each other:

**Dragon-Monkey-Rat Triangle**: Vision, resourcefulness, and intelligence
**Snake-Rooster-Ox Triangle**: Precision, dedication, and stability
**Horse-Dog-Tiger Triangle**: Energy, loyalty, and adventure
**Goat-Pig-Rabbit Triangle**: Creativity, compassion, and gentleness

## Layer 2: Bazi Compatibility — The Deeper Analysis

Zodiac compatibility is just the surface. Bazi compatibility examines **eight data points** per person:

### 1. Day Master Compatibility

Your Day Master (日主) is the most important compatibility factor. It represents your core elemental self and determines how your essential energy interacts with your partner's.

**Best Day Master pairings:**
- Wood ↔ Fire (Wood feeds Fire — natural support)
- Fire ↔ Earth (Fire creates Earth — transformation)
- Earth ↔ Metal (Earth produces Metal — grounding)
- Metal ↔ Water (Metal condenses Water — clarity)
- Water ↔ Wood (Water nourishes Wood — growth)

**Challenging Day Master pairings:**
- Wood ↔ Metal (Metal chops Wood — power struggle)
- Fire ↔ Water (Water extinguishes Fire — emotional tension)
- Earth ↔ Wood (Wood controls Earth — control issues)
- Metal ↔ Fire (Fire melts Metal — constant testing)
- Water ↔ Earth (Earth blocks Water — communication barriers)

### 2. Five Elements Complementarity

If your chart lacks an element and your partner has an abundance of it, this is a powerful compatibility signal. They bring what you need.

For example:
- Your chart is Fire-deficient + Partner has strong Fire = You feel energized around them
- Your chart is Water-heavy + Partner has strong Earth = Their stability grounds your emotions
- Your chart is Wood-light + Partner has strong Wood = They help you grow and expand

### 3. Spouse Palace Harmony

Your Day Branch (the branch under your Day Master) is your Spouse Palace. When it forms a harmonious combination with your partner's Spouse Palace, the relationship feels naturally comfortable.

### 4. Ten Gods Dynamics

The relationship between your Day Master and your partner's chart elements reveals how you function as a couple — who supports whom, where power dynamics exist, and what mutual growth looks like.

## How to Combine Zodiac and Bazi Compatibility

### Step 1: Quick Zodiac Screen

Check your zodiac signs first. If you're in a harmonious pair (六合), you have a strong foundation. If you're in a clash pair, don't worry — you'll need to work harder but it's absolutely possible.

### Step 2: Day Master Check

Compare your Day Masters. Complementary elements (Wood-Fire, Water-Wood, etc.) indicate natural support. Challenging elements (Wood-Metal, Fire-Water) require more conscious effort.

### Step 3: Five Elements Balance

Look at both charts' elemental profiles. The more complementary the gaps, the stronger the relationship potential.

### Step 4: Spouse Palace Analysis

Compare your Day Branches for harmonious combinations or clashes.

### Step 5: Synthesize the Full Picture

A relationship can have:
- **Zodiac clash but Day Master harmony** = Surface tension but deep compatibility
- **Zodiac harmony but Day Master clash** = Easy surface connection but underlying friction
- **Both harmonious** = Naturally smooth relationship
- **Both challenging** = Requires significant conscious work but can be deeply transformative
