## How Chinese Zodiac Yearly Fortune Works

Unlike Western astrology that focuses on your sun sign, Chinese zodiac fortune is determined by your **birth year animal**. Each year, the heavenly stem (天干) and earthly branch (地支) of the year create specific relationships — called **harmonies (合)** and **clashes (冲)** — with your birth animal.

- **Harmony years** tend to bring opportunities, smooth progress, and supportive energy.
- **Clash years** (犯太岁) bring change, obstacles, and the need for caution.
- **Fire Horse** specifically amplifies passion, speed, and intensity in all areas of life.

**Career:** Career changes are likely — possibly a job switch, relocation, or new business venture. The clash energy pushes you to break out of comfort zones. Embrace change rather than resist it.
