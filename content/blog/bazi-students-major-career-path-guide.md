# Bazi for Students: Choosing a Major and Career Path

Choosing a college major is one of the biggest decisions a young person makes. And most students make it with almost no information about who they really are.

Bazi can help. It reveals your natural talents, learning style, and career timing — so you can choose a path that aligns with your nature rather than fighting against it.

## Your Bazi Learning Style

| Day Master | Learning Style | Best Study Environment |
|-----------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Wood** | Hands-on, project-based | Collaborative, creative spaces |
| **Fire** | Visual, interactive | Bright, energetic, group settings |
| **Earth** | Step-by-step, practical | Structured, quiet, routine-based |
| **Metal** | Precise, systematic | Clean, organized, individual |
| **Water** | Deep, conceptual | Flexible, self-paced, reflective |

## Best Majors by Day Master

**Wood (Jia/Yi)** — Design, architecture, education, environmental science, creative writing, entrepreneurship

**Fire (Bing/Ding)** — Marketing, communications, entertainment, technology, public relations, performing arts

**Earth (Wu/Ji)** — Business, finance, real estate, healthcare administration, education, civil service

**Metal (Geng/Xin)** — Engineering, computer science, law, accounting, medicine (surgery), data science

**Water (Ren/Gui)** — Psychology, philosophy, international relations, logistics, research, spirituality studies

## When to Start Your Career

Your first Fortune Cycle after education is crucial. If it's favorable, start working immediately. If it's challenging, consider graduate school or skill development.
