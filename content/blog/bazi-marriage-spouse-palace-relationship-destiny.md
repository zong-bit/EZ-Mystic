# Bazi Marriage & Relationship Destiny: What Your Spouse Palace Reveals

One of the most fascinating aspects of Bazi (Four Pillars of Destiny) is the **Spouse Palace** (夫妻宫) — the Earthly Branch of your Day Pillar.

This single character reveals an extraordinary amount about your relationship patterns: the type of partner you're drawn to, the dynamics of your marriage, and the timing of major relationship events.

## What is the Spouse Palace?

Your Bazi chart has four pillars. The **Day Pillar** represents YOU — the Day Master (Heavenly Stem) is your core self, and the Earthly Branch is your **Spouse Palace**.

This branch represents:
- Your ideal partner's personality type
- The energy of your marriage/partnership
- Relationship challenges you'll face
- The timing of marriage

## Marriage Timing

Your Bazi chart doesn't just show WHO you'll marry — it shows WHEN.

**Early marriage indicators**: Spouse Palace in favorable branch + favorable Fortune Cycle for relationships in your 20s.

**Delayed marriage indicators**: Spouse Palace in clash + challenging relationship Fortune Cycles in 20s.

**Multiple marriage indicators**: Spouse Palace clashing with multiple branches in your chart.

**No marriage indicators**: Very rare — typically indicates a life path focused on career or spirituality.

## The Wealth Star Connection

For men, the **Direct Wealth Star** (正财) represents the wife. A strong Wealth Star in the correct position indicates good marriage fortune.

For women, the **Direct Power Star** (正官) represents the husband. A strong Power Star indicates good marriage prospects.

## Can Bazi Predict Divorce?

Bazi can show relationship challenges, but it doesn't predetermine divorce. Certain configurations indicate higher risk:

- Spouse Palace in **Direct Clash** (六冲) with your Year or Hour Pillar
- **Penalty combinations** (六害) in the Spouse Palace
- **Destructive combinations** (三刑) involving the Spouse Palace
- Strong **Rob Wealth** stars for men (indicates partner competition)
- Strong **Output stars** for women (indicates marriage stress)

But remember: Bazi shows tendencies, not certainties. Awareness of these patterns helps you work on the relationship proactively.
