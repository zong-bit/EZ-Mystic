## What Bing Wu (丙午) Means: Pure Fire Energy

**Bing (丙)** is Yang Fire — the sun. It represents visibility, charisma, warmth, generosity, and public presence.

**Wu (午)** is the Horse — one of the most active, energetic branches. It represents speed, independence, passion, movement, and freedom.

Together, Bing Wu creates: **Explosive energy, high visibility, rapid changes, intense passion, and a strong desire for freedom.**

### Why Pure Fire Matters in 2026

Most years have mixed elemental energy (e.g., 2025 was Yi Si — Wood Snake, where Wood produces Fire). But 2026 is **Fire on top of Fire** — the Stem and Branch are both Fire. This means:

- **Fire energy is at maximum intensity** — not diluted by other elements
- **Effects are amplified**: Fire's influence on every Day Master is stronger than in a mixed year
- **Polarizing**: Fire-loving charts thrive; fire-fearing charts feel overwhelmed

### Q: When does the Fire Horse year end?

### Q: How does 2026 compare to other Fire years?
Fire Horse (丙午) is considered one of the most intense fire years. Compared to Wood Snake (乙巳, 2025) or Fire Goat (丁未), the pure Fire of Bing Wu creates stronger effects in both directions — more opportunity for those who can handle it, more pressure for those who struggle with intensity.
