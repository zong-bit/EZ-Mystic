# Bazi Lucky Direction Guide — How to Find Your Most Auspicious Directions Using Kua Number

In the world of Chinese metaphysics, **direction is not just geography — it's energy**. The ancient Bagua Eight Mansions (八宅) system has been used for over 2,000 years to determine which directions are most auspicious for each person based on their birth year and gender.

While Bazi (八字) primarily uses your Day Master element to determine favorable directions, the **Kua Number (命卦)** system offers a complementary approach — one that maps eight distinct energy types to the compass directions, each carrying its own fortune for wealth, health, career, and relationships.

In this guide, you'll learn how to calculate your Kua Number, interpret the eight directions it reveals for you, and apply this knowledge to real-life decisions like where to sleep, work, travel, or even live.

---

## What Is the Kua Number System?

The **Kua Number (命卦, Mìng Guà)** is a single-digit number from 1 to 9 derived from your birth year and gender. It corresponds to one of the **Eight Trigrams (八卦)** of I Ching philosophy, and each trigram has a unique set of **four auspicious (吉)** and **four inauspicious (凶)** directions.

The system divides all people into two groups:

| Group | Kua Numbers | Trigrams | Core Energy |
|-------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **East Group (东四命)** | 1, 3, 4, 9 | Kan (Water), Zhen (Wood), Xun (Wood), Li (Fire) | Growth, expansion, vitality |
| **West Group (西四命)** | 2, 6, 7, 8 | Kun (Earth), Qian (Metal), Dui (Metal), Gen (Earth) | Stability, structure, grounding |

**Key principle**: East Group people are most fortunate in the four cardinal directions (North, South, East, Southeast). West Group people thrive in the other four (Southwest, Northwest, West, Northeast).

### Kua Number 5 — The Special Case

When the calculation yields **Kua 5**, gender determines your actual trigram:
- **Male Kua 5** → maps to Kun (坤, Earth) — same as West Group Kua 2
- **Female Kua 5** → maps to Gen (艮, Earth) — same as West Group Kua 8

## How to Calculate Your Kua Number (Step by Step)

The calculation uses only your **birth year** and **gender**. Here's the exact formula:

### Step 1: Reduce Your Birth Year to a Single Digit

Take the last two digits of your birth year and add them together repeatedly until you get a single digit.

**Example 1**: Born in **1985** → last two digits = `8 + 5 = 13` → `1 + 3 = **4**`

**Example 2**: Born in **1978** → `7 + 8 = 15` → `1 + 5 = **6**`

### Step 2: Apply the Gender Formula

| Gender | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| **Male** (born in Yang years*) | 11 − reduced digit |
| **Female** (born in Yin years*) | reduced digit + 5 |

> *Yang and Yin year classification: In the traditional Chinese calendar, years alternate between Yang (阳) and Yin (阴). For simplicity in modern application, most practitioners use the simplified formulas below that work for all years.

### Simplified Universal Formulas

For ease of use, here are the formulas that work for **any** birth year:

| Gender | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| **Male** (born 1900–1999) | 11 − (sum of last two digits, reduced to single digit) |
| **Male** (born 2000–2099) | 10 − (sum of last two digits, reduced to single digit) — if result is 5 → use 2 |
| **Female** (born 1900–1999) | reduced digit + 5 — if result > 9, reduce again |
| **Female** (born 2000–2099) | reduced digit + 4 — if result > 9, reduce again; if result is 5 → use 8 |

### Worked Examples

**Example A — Male, born 1985**:
- Last two digits: `8 + 5 = 13` → `1 + 3 = **4**`
- Formula: `11 − 4 = **7**`
- Kua Number: **7 (兑 Dui · Metal)** → West Group

**Example B — Female, born 1992**:
- Last two digits: `9 + 2 = 11` → `1 + 1 = **2**`
- Formula: `2 + 5 = **7**`
- Kua Number: **7 (兑 Dui · Metal)** → West Group

**Example C — Male, born 1970**:
- Last two digits: `7 + 0 = **7**`
- Formula: `11 − 7 = **4**`
- Kua Number: **4 (巽 Xun · Wood)** → East Group

**Example D — Female, born 1985**:
- Last two digits: `8 + 5 = 13` → `1 + 3 = **4**`
- Formula: `4 + 5 = **9**`
- Kua Number: **9 (离 Li · Fire)** → East Group

## The Eight Directions — Four Auspicious, Four Inauspicious

Every Kua Number produces a unique arrangement of eight directions. Each direction carries one of **eight energy types**:

### The Four Auspicious Energies (吉方)

| Energy | Chinese | Pinyin | English | Best For |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|----------|
| **Sheng Qi** (生气) | Shēng Qì | Vitality & Wealth | Career success, business deals, financial growth, conception |
| **Tian Yi** (天医) | Tiān Yī | Health & Healing | Bedrooms, recovery spaces, wellness activities, health decisions |
| **Yan Nian** (延年) | Yán Nián | Longevity & Relationships | Office desks, negotiations, marriage planning, family harmony |
| **Fu Wei** (伏位) | Fú Wèi | Stability & Calm | Meditation, study, daily routines, personal reflection |

### The Four Inauspicious Energies (凶方)

| Energy | Chinese | Pinyin | English | Best For / Avoid |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Huo Hai** (祸害) | Huò Hài | Minor Obstacles | Avoid important decisions; good for grounding exercises |
| **Liu Sha** (六煞) | Liù Shà | Emotional Turbulence | Avoid social events; good for artistic contemplation alone |
| **Jue Ming** (绝命) | Jué Mìng | Loss & Challenges | Avoid entirely for major life decisions; use only for exercise to transform negative energy |
| **Wu Gui** (五鬼) | Wǔ Guǐ | Unpredictable Change | Avoid for stability; good for creative breakthroughs and innovation |

> **Important**: Inauspicious directions don't mean "bad luck" — they indicate areas where your energy is challenged. With awareness and compensation, you can still succeed in these directions while minimizing risk.

## Kua Number 5 — The Special Case Explained

When your calculation yields **Kua 5**, the person doesn't have their own trigram. Instead, they "borrow" another:

| Gender | Borrowed Trigram | Same As | Group |
|--------|-----------------|---------|-------|
| **Male** Kua 5 | Kun (坤 · Earth) | West Group Kua 2 | West |
| **Female** Kua 5 | Gen (艮 · Earth) | West Group Kua 8 | West |

This special handling exists because in traditional Chinese cosmology, the number 5 represents the **center** — the pivot point around which all other energies rotate. The center has no direction of its own, so it must align with an existing trigram based on gender.

## How to Apply Your Directions in Daily Life

### 1. Sleeping Direction (Bed Headboard)

Your **Tian Yi (Health)** direction is ideal for where you place your headboard. This aligns your body with healing energy during the 7-9 hours you spend unconscious each night.

> **Example**: If your Kua Number is 1 and Tian Yi points Northeast, position your bed so your head points toward the northeast corner of your room.

### 2. Working Direction (Desk Facing)

Your **Sheng Qi (Wealth)** or **Yan Nian (Career)** direction determines where you should face while working. Facing Sheng Qi attracts new opportunities; facing Yan Nian sustains career stability.

> **Practical tip**: If you can't reposition your desk, even a small compass on your monitor pointing toward your auspicious direction creates a psychological anchor that enhances focus and intention.

### 3. Home Entrance Orientation

The main door of your home determines how energy enters your living space. A favorable entrance direction supports the entire household's fortune.

> **For renters**: If you can't choose your apartment door, use your Kua direction for the room where you spend the most time — typically the bedroom or home office.

### 4. Travel and Relocation

When planning a significant move, business trip, or relocation:
- **Sheng Qi direction** is best for career moves and starting new ventures
- **Tian Yi direction** is ideal for health-related relocations or recovery periods
- **Avoid Jue Ming direction** for major life transitions

### 5. Conception and Family Planning

In traditional Chinese practice, the Sheng Qi direction is considered the most auspicious for conception. Couples trying to conceive are advised to sleep with their heads pointing toward this direction during fertile windows.

> **Note**: This complements — not replaces — medical fertility guidance. Many couples use both Bazi TTC timing and Kua direction alignment together.

## Combining Kua Number with Bazi Directions

The Kua Number system and Bazi Day Master approach are **complementary**, not competing. Here's how to use them together:

| Approach | Basis | Granularity | Best For |
|----------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Kua Number (八宅)** | Birth year + gender | 8 specific directions per person | Daily life orientation, home layout, travel decisions |
| **Bazi Day Master** | Full birth chart + element balance | Elemental direction preferences (East/West/South/North) | Long-term life strategy, career alignment, elemental harmony |

**The integrated approach**:
1. Use **Kua Number** for room-level decisions (bed position, desk facing)
2. Use **Bazi Day Master** for macro-level decisions (city selection, region relocation)
3. Where they align — that direction gets double emphasis

> **Example**: If your Kua Number says Sheng Qi is Southeast AND your Bazi Day Master needs Fire (which corresponds to South), then the **Southeast direction** gets maximum priority — it's favorable from both systems.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Using Solar Year vs. Lunar Year Confusion

The Kua Number traditionally uses the **Chinese lunar calendar**, not the Gregorian calendar. However, in modern practice:
- If you were born **before Chinese New Year** (typically January 21–February 3), use the **previous year's** Kua Number
- If born **after Chinese New Year**, use your Gregorian birth year

### 2. Ignoring Gender Differences

Male and female formulas produce **different Kua Numbers** for the same birth year. Using the wrong gender formula gives you a completely incorrect direction map.

### 3. Treating Inauspicious Directions as "Cursed"

A Jue Ming (绝命) direction doesn't mean you'll fail if you work there. It means the energy is **challenging** — like climbing a steep hill instead of walking flat. Success requires more effort, awareness, and preparation.

### 4. Applying Directions Without Considering Practical Constraints

Your ideal Sheng Qi direction might be occupied by a bathroom or hallway. In such cases:
- Use the **next best direction** (Tian Yi or Yan Nian) as your primary
- Apply elemental cures in the unfavorable direction (e.g., place a water feature to transform Jue Ming's metal energy)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Kua Number in feng shui?
A Kua Number (命卦) is a single-digit number from 1 to 9 calculated from your birth year and gender. It identifies which of the Eight Trigrams you belong to, and each trigram has a unique set of four lucky directions and four unlucky directions. It's the core tool in the Bagua Eight Mansions (八宅) feng shui system for directional energy.

### How accurate is Kua Number direction calculation?
Kua Number directions have been used for over 2,000 years in Chinese feng shui practice. While scientific validation is limited, many practitioners report significant improvements in sleep quality, work productivity, and relationship harmony after aligning living spaces with their Kua directions. It works best when combined with other Bazi and feng shui methods rather than used in isolation.

### Do Kua directions change every year like annual feng shui?
No — your Kua Number and its associated directions are **fixed for life**. They don't change with annual flying stars or monthly transits. However, the effectiveness of each direction can be enhanced or weakened by annual energy shifts, which is why advanced practitioners layer Kua directions over the 9-Year Flying Star system for year-by-year refinement.

### What's the difference between Kua Number directions and Bazi element directions?
Kua Number directions come from the Eight Mansions (八宅) system and are based on your birth year and gender — giving you 8 specific directional energies. Bazi element directions come from your full birth chart and Day Master, showing which elemental directions (East/West/South/North) support your overall balance. They use different methodologies and often point to similar but not identical directions — using both gives you a more complete picture.

### Can my lucky direction be the same as someone else's?
Yes — people born in different years can end up with the same Kua Number. For example, males born in 1985 and 2001 both have Kua Number 7. However, because Bazi element directions depend on your full birth chart (month, day, and hour pillars), two people with the same Kua Number may still have different Bazi-favorable directions.

### What should I do if my apartment's door faces an inauspicious direction?
Don't panic. Inauspicious directions are challenges, not curses. Here are practical remedies: (1) Use the next-best direction as your primary orientation — for example, if Sheng Qi is blocked, use Tian Yi. (2) Place elemental cures in the unfavorable direction — like a small water feature to soften challenging energy. (3) Focus on what you can control: your desk facing, bed position, and where you spend focused time.
