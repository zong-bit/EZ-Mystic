- Transformation happens through sustained warmth, not explosive energy
- The year rewards patience, attention to detail, and nurturing energy

### Wei Earth (未) — The Fertile Goat

The Goat branch carries Ji Earth (己), Ding Fire (丁), and Yi Wood (乙) as hidden stems. This creates a complex energy of **nurturing fertility** — the kind of earth that grows flowers, feeds crops, and sustains life.

**What this means for 2027:**

- Relationships and community are central themes

- The year rewards those who build and nurture rather than conquer

### The Ding-Wei Combination: Fire Nourishing Earth

When Ding Fire sits on Wei Earth, the Fire produces Earth — creating a year where **foundation-building, relationship-cultivation, and creative expression** are the dominant themes. The energy is warm, supportive, and deeply rooted.
