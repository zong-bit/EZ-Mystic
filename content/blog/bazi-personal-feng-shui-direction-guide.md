## Why Direction Matters in Bazi

Every person has a unique directional energy profile based on their birth chart. Unlike generic feng shui advice that tells everyone to face the same "lucky direction," Bazi personalizes this based on your **Day Master** and **favorable elements**.

This guide shows you how to find your true lucky directions — the ones that actually work for YOUR chart, not someone else's.

## The Five Elements and Their Directions

Each of the five elements governs specific compass directions:

| Element | Direction | Compass Degrees |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Wood** (木) | East & Southeast | 70°–165° |
| **Fire** (火) | South | 140°–220° |
| **Earth** (土) | Northeast, Southwest, Center | 15°–40°, 150°–175°, and central area |
| **Metal** (金) | West & Northwest | 245°–305° |
| **Water** (水) | North | 325°–40° |

Your favorable elements determine which directions bring you luck. If your chart needs more Fire, the South direction amplifies that energy — helping with career recognition and social connections.

## Practical Applications of Your Directions

### Career Direction
Sit facing your favorable direction during important meetings, interviews, or when making strategic decisions. If South is lucky for you and your office faces south, that's a natural advantage — amplify it by placing red or orange accents in the southern part of your workspace.

### Wealth Direction
Your wealth direction is typically related to your favorable element's associated direction. If Metal (West) is lucky for you, the West sector of your home or office should be kept clean and well-lit. Consider placing a metal wind chime or coin vase in the west to activate wealth energy.

### Love and Relationship Direction
The relationship direction varies by chart, but generally:
- **Wood** types benefit from East (growth and harmony)
- **Fire** types benefit from South (passion and visibility)  
- **Earth** types benefit from Northeast/Southwest (stability)
- **Metal** types benefit from West/Northwest (communication and clarity)
- **Water** types benefit from North (intuition and flow)

### Health Direction
When you're feeling unwell or need to make health-related decisions, facing your favorable direction can support recovery. Many traditional practitioners recommend sleeping with your head pointing toward one of your lucky directions — typically the strongest among them.

## Seasonal Direction Rotation

Your luckiest direction changes with seasons because the elemental energy shifts:

| Season | Dominant Element | Best Direction |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| Spring (Mar–May) | Wood | East/Southeast |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Fire | South |
| Late Summer/Transition (Sep) | Earth | Northeast/Southwest |
| Autumn (Oct–Dec) | Metal | West/Northwest |
| Winter (Jan–Feb) | Water | North |

Combine your personal favorable direction with the seasonal dominant element for maximum effect. For example, if South is lucky for you and it's summer (Fire season), that double Fire energy can be very powerful — but also needs balance to avoid excess.

## Common Mistakes People Make with Directional Feng Shui

### 1. Using Generic "Lucky Directions"
Most feng shui guides give the same directions to everyone based on birth year. This ignores your complete chart and is like wearing someone else's prescription glasses — it might partially work, but you're missing the precision.

### 2. Ignoring Your Unfavorable Directions
Knowing which directions are **unlucky** is just as important. If North (Water) is unfavorable for you, avoid making major life decisions when facing north, and keep that area of your home relatively quiet.

### 3. Overcomplicating the Setup
You don't need to rearrange your entire home. Start with small adjustments: which way you face at work, how you position your bed, or where you place certain objects.

### 4. Expecting Instant Results
Directional energy works like compound interest — gradual, cumulative effects over months and years. Don't expect a promotion the day you face south; instead, notice whether opportunities seem to flow more naturally over time.
