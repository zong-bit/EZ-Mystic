## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these monthly predictions accurate for everyone?

These predictions are based on your **zodiac year animal**. For more precise monthly guidance, your full Bazi chart (including Day Master, Luck Pillars, and specific chart combinations) provides a much more nuanced picture.

### Should I avoid all actions during "caution months"?

Not necessarily. Caution months don't mean "do nothing" — they mean "proceed with awareness." Focus on preparation, research, and low-risk activities. Save major decisions for favorable months when possible.

### How do I find my lunar birth month?

The Chinese lunar calendar doesn't align with the Gregorian calendar. Your lunar birth month depends on the specific lunar date of your birth. Use a Bazi calculator to determine your exact lunar month and its associated energy.
