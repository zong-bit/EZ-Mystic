## Introduction

When people ask about Chinese astrology, they often think of zodiac signs and horoscopes. But **Bazi (八字)** goes far deeper. It's a system that uses your exact birth time to create a unique cosmic snapshot — a destiny code that reveals patterns about your personality, career, relationships, and life cycles.

One of the most common questions from beginners is: **how do you actually calculate a Bazi chart?** The answer involves two parallel systems that have been refined over 2,000 years: the Heavenly Stems (天干) and the Earthly Branches (地支).

## The Heavenly Stems (天干)

The Heavenly Stems are a cycle of 10 characters, each associated with one of the Five Elements:

| Stem | Element | Yin/Yang | Symbolism |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| 甲 (Jiǎ) | Wood | Yang | Towering tree |
| 乙 (Yǐ) | Wood | Yin | Vine or flower |
| 丙 (Bǐng) | Fire | Yang | The sun |
| 丁 (Dīng) | Fire | Yin | Candle flame |
| 戊 (Wù) | Earth | Yang | Mountain |
| 己 (Jǐ) | Earth | Yin | Garden soil |
| 庚 (Gēng) | Metal | Yang | Raw ore |
| 辛 (Xīn) | Metal | Yin | Jewelry |
| 壬 (Rén) | Water | Yang | Ocean |
| 癸 (Guǐ) | Water | Yin | Rain/dew |

Each stem represents not just an element, but a **quality** of that element. Yang stems are expansive and outward-moving; Yin stems are contained and inward-focused.

## The Earthly Branches (地支)

The Earthly Branches are a cycle of 12 characters, each corresponding to a zodiac animal and an element:

Each branch also contains hidden Heavenly Stems (藏干), which adds another layer of depth to Bazi analysis.

## How to Calculate Your Bazi Chart

Calculating a Bazi chart requires four pieces of information:

1. **Birth year** → Year Pillar (年柱)
2. **Birth month** → Month Pillar (月柱)
3. **Birth day** → Day Pillar (日柱)
4. **Birth hour** → Hour Pillar (时柱)

### Year Pillar

The Chinese New Year doesn't align with January 1st. In Bazi, the year changes at **Li Chun (立春)**, typically February 4th. If you were born between January 1st and February 3rd, your Bazi year is still the previous year.

The Year Stem is calculated using the formula: **(last digit of birth year - 3) mod 10**, mapped to the Heavenly Stems. The Year Branch uses: **(birth year - 3) mod 12**, mapped to the Earthly Branches.

### Month Pillar

The Month Pillar depends on the **solar terms (节气)**, not the lunar calendar. Each month starts at a specific solar term. The Month Stem is determined by the Year Stem using the **五虎遁 (Five Tigers Escape)** method.

### Day Pillar

The Day Pillar uses a continuous count from a known reference date. There's a mathematical formula (based on the **干支纪日法**) that can calculate any day's Stem-Branch pair without counting day by day.

### Hour Pillar

The Hour Branch is determined by the birth hour (each 2-hour window = one branch). The Hour Stem is calculated from the Day Stem using the **五鼠遁 (Five Rats Escape)** method.
