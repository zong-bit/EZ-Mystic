# The 10 Gods in Bazi: Complete Guide to Wealth, Power, and Creativity Stars

If your Day Master is the "main character" in your Bazi chart, the **10 Gods (Shi Shen/十神)** are the supporting cast — the relationships, energies, and dynamics that shape your life.

Each of the other Heavenly Stems in your chart relates to your Day Master in one of 10 ways. These relationships reveal your:

- **Wealth potential** (how you make money)
- **Career power** (your authority and advancement)
- **Creativity and expression** (how you output energy)
- **Resources and support** (who and what supports you)

## The 10 Gods at a Glance

The 10 Gods are divided into two groups based on Yin/Yang polarity:

| Category | Same Polarity | Opposite Polarity |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Resource** | Direct Resource (正印) | Indirect Resource (偏印) |
| **Wealth** | Direct Wealth (正财) | Indirect Wealth (偏财) |
| **Power** | Direct Power (正官) | Indirect Power (偏官) |
| **Output** | Direct Output (食神) | Indirect Output (伤官) |
| **Peer** | Friend (比肩) | Rob Wealth (劫财) |

## The 5 Categories Explained

### 1. Resource Stars (印星) — Support and Knowledge

**Direct Resource (Zheng Yin/正印)** — Opposite polarity
- Caregivers, teachers, mentors
- Formal education, qualifications
- Protection and support
- Strong: You have good mentors, academic success
- Weak: Lack of support, self-taught

**Indirect Resource (Pian Yin/偏印)** — Same polarity
- Unconventional knowledge (occult, astrology)
- Independent learning
- Creative problem-solving
- Strong: You're intuitive, resourceful, think outside the box
- Weak: You prefer structured learning

### 2. Wealth Stars (财星) — Money and Resources

**Direct Wealth (Zheng Cai/正财)** — Opposite polarity
- Steady salary, regular income
- Spouse (traditionally)
- Financial stability
- Strong: Good earning ability, financially responsible
- Weak: Difficulty with consistent income

**Indirect Wealth (Pian Cai/偏财)** — Same polarity
- Windfall, investments, side income
- Business profits
- Financial risk-taking
- Strong: Entrepreneurial ability, good with investments
- Weak: Risk-averse, prefers stable income

### 3. Power Stars (官星) — Authority and Career

**Direct Power (Zheng Guan/正官)** — Opposite polarity
- Official positions
- Management authority
- Reputation and status
- Strong: Natural leader, respected, follows rules
- Weak: Struggles with authority or leadership

**Indirect Power (Pian Guan/偏官)** — Same polarity
- Entrepreneurship, military, competition
- Taking charge independently
- Overcoming challenges
- Strong: Bold, competitive, thrives under pressure
- Weak: Avoids confrontation

### 4. Output Stars (食伤) — Creativity and Expression

**Direct Output (Shi Shen/食神)** — Opposite polarity
- Calm creativity, cooking, art
- Teaching, sharing knowledge
- Enjoyment of life
- Strong: Talented, enjoys life, generous
- Weak: Lacks creative outlet

**Indirect Output (Shang Guan/伤官)** — Same polarity
- Intense creativity, innovation
- Public speaking, performance
- Challenging authority
- Strong: Brilliant, expressive, innovative
- Weak: Difficulty expressing ideas

### 5. Peer Stars (比劫) — Relationships and Competition

**Friend (Bi Jian/比肩)** — Same polarity
- Siblings, close friends
- Partners at same level
- Competition
- Strong: Good support network, competitive
- Weak: Prefers working alone

**Rob Wealth (Jie Cai/劫财)** — Opposite polarity
- Colleagues, acquaintances
- Financial competition
- Need to share resources
- Strong: Good network, but may lose money to others
- Weak: Doesn't attract support

## How to Read Your 10 Gods

When analyzing your Bazi chart:

**1. Count how many of each category appear**

**2. Evaluate their strength** (Earthly Branches can contain hidden Heavenly Stems)

**3. Check their position**
- Wealth Star in your Career Pillar = Good earning years
- Power Star in your Month Pillar = Leadership potential
- Output Star in your Hour Pillar = Talents manifest in later life

**4. Look for combinations**
- Strong Wealth + Weak Day Master = You have money-making ideas but lack energy to execute
- Strong Power + Strong Resource = Career success through education and mentorship
- Strong Output + Weak Resource = Creative talent but needs more knowledge

## The Most Important Gods for Your Life

### For Career
- Direct/Indirect Power stars = Career authority
- Direct Wealth = Stable income career
- Indirect Wealth = Entrepreneurial career

### For Relationships
- Direct Wealth = Spouse (for men)
- Direct Power = Husband (for women)
- Direct Resource = Good relationship mentors

### For Wealth
- Direct Wealth = Salary earner
- Indirect Wealth = Business/investment income
- Rob Wealth = Need to manage shared finances carefully

### For Creativity
- Direct Output = Calm, steady creative output
- Indirect Output = Intense, innovative creativity

## The Dynamic Balance

A "good" Bazi chart isn't about having all 10 Gods — it's about the right balance:

- **Too many Resource stars**: May be overprotected, lack independence
- **Too many Wealth stars**: Financial instability, too focused on money
- **Too many Power stars**: Under too much pressure, rule-bound
- **Too many Output stars**: Scattered energy, too many ideas
- **Too many Peer stars**: Too much competition, difficulty saving

The ideal chart has **2-3 of each relevant category**, with the majority supporting your Day Master.
