# The 10 Day Masters of Bazi — Complete Personality Comparison Chart

In Western astrology, you have one sun sign that defines your core personality. In **Bazi (八字)**, you have a **Day Master** (日主) — the Heavenly Stem of your birth day — that serves an equivalent but far more nuanced role.

There are **10 Day Masters**, each representing a unique combination of element and polarity (Yin/Yang). Understanding which one you are is the first step to reading your Bazi chart — and comparing all 10 side by side reveals patterns that help you understand not just yourself, but how different types interact with the world.

This comparison chart covers all 10 Day Masters in a format designed for easy reference and social sharing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Day Masters are there in Bazi?**
Exactly 10, corresponding to the 10 Heavenly Stems. Each of the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) has a Yang form and a Yin form: Jia/Yi Wood, Bing/Ding Fire, Wu/Ji Earth, Geng/Xin Metal, Ren/Gui Water.

**Is a Yang Day Master better than a Yin Day Master?**
No. Neither is inherently superior — they represent different approaches to life. Yang stems tend toward directness and outward expression, while Yin stems tend toward subtlety and strategic adaptability. The best Day Master depends on your full chart context, not the polarity alone.

**Can my Day Master change?**
No. Your Day Master is fixed at birth — it's the Heavenly Stem of your birth day and never changes. What does change are your Fortune Cycles (大运), which represent 10-year periods of shifting elemental influences that interact with your Day Master.

**How is the Day Master different from my Chinese Zodiac sign?**
Your Zodiac sign only uses your birth year (one pillar). Your Day Master uses your exact birth day — specifically the Heavenly Stem. Two people born in the same Year of the Dragon can have completely different Day Masters, meaning they'll experience life very differently.

**What's the difference between Jia Wood and Yi Wood?**
Jia (甲) is Yang Wood — like a mighty tree reaching for the sky: ambitious, direct, and protective. Yi (乙) is Yin Wood — like a flexible vine: adaptable, strategic, and able to thrive in any environment. Both are Wood but express it very differently.

**Why do some Bazi calculators show different Day Masters?**
The calculation depends on accurate birth time, correct calendar conversion (solar vs. lunar), and proper timezone handling. Make sure your calculator uses the standard Chinese calendar formulas with true solar time corrections.
