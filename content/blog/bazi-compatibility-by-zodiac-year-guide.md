## Chinese Zodiac Compatibility: The First Layer of Bazi Love Matching

Your Chinese zodiac sign — determined by your birth year — is the most widely known aspect of Bazi astrology. But here's what most people don't realize: **your zodiac sign is only one of eight data points** in your complete Bazi compatibility profile.

That said, the year pillar (your zodiac sign) still plays an important role in relationship dynamics, especially in how your families perceive the match and in long-term cultural alignment.

## The 12 Zodiac Signs: Compatibility Groups

In Bazi and Chinese astrology, the 12 zodiac signs form specific compatibility groups based on harmonious combinations and challenging clashes.

### 🐀 Rat (子 Zi) — The Intelligent Strategist

**Best Matches:** Ox (丑), Dragon (辰), Monkey (申)
**Challenging Matches:** Horse (午), Goat (未)

Rats are clever, adaptable, and emotionally sensitive. They match best with Ox's stability, Dragon's boldness, and Monkey's wit. The Rat-Horse clash is one of the six major oppositions in Chinese astrology.

### 🐂 Ox (丑 Chou) — The Steady Provider

**Best Matches:** Rat (子), Snake (巳), Rooster (酉)
**Challenging Matches:** Horse (午), Goat (未), Dog (戌)

Oxen value loyalty and consistency. They form powerful bonds with Rat's intelligence, Snake's wisdom, and Rooster's attention to detail.

### 🐅 Tiger (寅 Yin) — The Bold Pioneer

**Best Matches:** Pig (亥), Horse (午), Dog (戌)
**Challenging Matches:** Monkey (申), Snake (巳)

Tigers are courageous and independent. Their best matches are Pig's gentle acceptance, Horse's shared energy, and Dog's loyal support.

### 🐇 Rabbit (卯 Mao) — The Graceful Diplomat

**Best Matches:** Dog (戌), Goat (未), Pig (亥)
**Challenging Matches:** Rooster (酉), Dragon (辰)

Rabbits are gentle, refined, and value harmony. They thrive with Dog's devotion, Goat's creativity, and Pig's warmth.

### 🐉 Dragon (辰 Chen) — The Ambitious Leader
