## The Five Elements Number System (河洛数理)

In Chinese numerology, every digit from 0-9 is associated with one of the Five Elements. This system, rooted in ancient **Hetu (河图)** and **Luoshu (洛书)** cosmology, maps numbers to elemental energies:

### Basic Number-to-Element Mapping

| Number | Element | Yin/Yang | Symbolic Meaning |
|--------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Water | Yang | Origin, leadership, new beginnings |
| 2 | Earth | Yin | Nurturing, support, stability |
| 3 | Wood | Yang | Growth, expansion, creativity |
| 4 | Wood | Yin | Structure, refinement, communication |
| 5 | Earth | Yang | Power, authority, transformation |
| 6 | Metal | Yin | Authority, protection, wealth accumulation |
| 7 | Metal | Yang | Communication, action, social energy |
| 8 | Earth | Yin | Wealth, prosperity, material success |
| 9 | Fire | Yang | Fame, recognition, peak achievement |
| 0 | Earth | Neutral | Potential, emptiness, beginning or ending |

### Why Two Numbers Share Each Element

You may notice that Wood appears twice (3 and 4), Earth three times (2, 5, 8), Metal appears twice (6 and 7), Water once (1), and Fire only once as Yang (9). This reflects the **Yin-Yang distribution** in Hetu numerology:

- **Yang numbers (1, 3, 5, 7, 9)**: Active, outward-moving energy
- **Yin numbers (2, 4, 6, 8)**: Receptive, stabilizing energy
- **0**: Neutral — represents the void from which all numbers emerge

> **Practical tip**: When analyzing your phone number, treat 0 as neutral or as Earth (grounding). It doesn't clash with anything but also doesn't strongly support any element.

> Example: Phone number ending in 135-7842

This single-digit result represents the overall elemental "vibe" of your number. Use this as a secondary check — it should ideally support your favorable elements.

### Strategy 3: Avoiding Harmful Number Combinations

Certain number combinations are considered inauspicious regardless of your Bazi:

| Combination | Traditional Meaning |
|------------|-------------------|

| 250 | Slang for "foolish" — avoid in personal numbers |
| 666 | Internet slang for "awesome" but traditionally represents the Three Realms — mixed meanings |
| 404 | "Not found" — modern association to avoid |

> **Modern perspective**: If you're not deeply superstitious, the number 4 is fine — many successful people use it. But if you want to minimize any potential negative psychological association, prefer alternatives like 7 (sounds like "rise" in some dialects) or 8.
