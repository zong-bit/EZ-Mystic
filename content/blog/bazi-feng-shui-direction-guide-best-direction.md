# Bazi & Feng Shui Direction Guide: How to Choose Your Best Direction

In both Bazi and Feng Shui, **direction matters** — a lot.

Your Bazi chart reveals which directions support your energy and which drain it. Aligning your life with your favorable directions can have a surprisingly powerful effect on your career, relationships, and health.

## How Directions Are Determined

Your favorable directions are calculated from your **Day Master** and the Five Elements in your chart:

| Element | Favorable Direction | Neutral Direction | Unfavorable |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Wood** 🌳 | East, Southeast | South, North | West, Southwest |
| **Fire** 🔥 | South, Southeast | East, Southwest | North, West |
| **Earth** 🏔️ | Center, Southwest, Northeast | West, South | East, North |
| **Metal** ⚔️ | West, Northwest | Northeast, Center | South, East |
| **Water** 💧 | North, East | Northwest, West | Southwest, South |

But this is just the starting point. Your individual chart may have different favorable directions based on your specific element balance.

| **Southeast** | Wood | Wealth, abundance | Financial growth |
| **South** | Fire | Fame, recognition, passion | Career advancement, visibility |

## How to Use Your Favorable Directions

### 1. Sleep Direction
Sleeping with your head pointing toward your favorable direction improves sleep quality and daily energy.

**If your favorable direction is East**: Sleep with your head pointing East — this aligns you with growth energy.

### 2. Work Direction
Sit facing your favorable direction when working or studying.

**If your favorable direction is South**: Position your desk so you face South. This enhances focus and recognition.

### 3. Entrance Direction
If possible, choose a home or office with your favorable direction as the main entrance.

### 4. Travel Direction
Travel to your favorable direction for important trips — job interviews, business deals, relationship meetings.

## When Directions Clash

Sometimes your favorable direction for one life area clashes with another. For example:

- Career (North) is water, but your Day Master needs Fire (South)
- Health (East) is wood, but your chart needs Metal (West)

**Solution**: Prioritize your Day Master's needs first. Then use secondary cures to balance conflicting energies.

## Practical Examples

**Example 1**: Jia Wood Day Master with strong Wood element
- Favorable: East (strengthens Wood), South (feeds Fire)
- Unfavorable: West (Metal cuts Wood)
- Action: Sleep East, work facing South, avoid West-facing entrances

**Example 2**: Bing Fire Day Master with missing Fire
- Favorable: South (adds Fire), East (Wood creates Fire)
- Unfavorable: North (Water extinguishes Fire)
- Action: Add red to South area of home, sleep East (Wood feeds Fire), avoid North-facing desk
