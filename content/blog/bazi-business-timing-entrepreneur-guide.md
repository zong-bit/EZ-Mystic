# Bazi for Business: Timing Your Entrepreneurial Moves

Every entrepreneur knows that timing is everything. A great idea launched at the wrong time fails; a mediocre idea launched at the right time succeeds.

Bazi gives you an unfair advantage: it shows you exactly when your favorable Fortune Cycles are. Instead of guessing whether now is the right time to start that business, raise that round, or make that pivot — your chart tells you.

## The Entrepreneur's Bazi Checklist

Before starting a business, check these elements in your chart:

### 1. Do You Have Entrepreneurial Energy?

Your Day Master should be supported by the elements it needs:

| If Your Day Master Is | You Need |
|----------------------|----------|
| Weak | A strong Fortune Cycle OR a co-founder who complements you |
| Strong | The right industry based on your Wealth Star |
| Balanced | Perfect for entrepreneurship — you adapt well |

### 2. Check Your Wealth Star

- **Indirect Wealth (偏财)** dominant: Natural entrepreneur. You see opportunities others miss.
- **Direct Wealth (正财)** dominant: Better in traditional business or high-level employment first.
- **Wealth Star clashing**: Business partnerships need legal protection.

### 3. Check Your Power Star

- **Indirect Power (偏官)** strong: Born for entrepreneurship and competition.
- **Direct Power (正官)** strong: Better in established organizations or regulated industries.

## The Four Business Phases in Bazi

### Phase 1: Incubation (When Elements Are Low)

**Characteristics**: Low energy, testing ideas, no rush
**Do**: Research, build relationships, learn skills, validate ideas
**Don't**: Quit your job, invest heavily, scale prematurely
**Duration**: 1-3 years depending on your Fortune Cycle

### Phase 2: Launch (When Favorable Fortune Cycle Begins)

**Characteristics**: Energy returns, opportunities appear, doors open
**Do**: Launch, raise initial capital, build MVP, hire first team
**Don't**: Wait for perfection, ignore the momentum
**Signs**: Your favorable element's year in your Fortune Cycle

### Phase 3: Growth (Peak Fortune Cycle)

**Characteristics**: Rapid growth, recognition, abundance
**Do**: Scale aggressively, raise Series A/B, expand team, enter new markets
**Don't**: Get overconfident, neglect cash reserves, ignore competitors
**Duration**: The full 10-year Fortune Cycle

### Phase 4: Consolidation (When Cycle Shifts)

**Characteristics**: Growth slows, challenges increase
**Do**: Consolidate, optimize, build reserves, prepare for next cycle
**Don't**: Over-leverage, expand into unrelated areas, ignore warning signs
**Next step**: Wait for the next favorable cycle

## Fundraising by Element

**Wood years**: Best for fundraising for creative, education, or health businesses
**Fire years**: Best for fundraising for visible, consumer-facing businesses
**Earth years**: Best for fundraising for stable, real estate, or resource businesses
**Metal years**: Best for fundraising for tech, finance, or precision businesses
**Water years**: Best for fundraising for logistics, trading, or communication businesses

## Common Entrepreneurial Chart Patterns

### The Serial Entrepreneur
Multiple Wealth Stars scattered across pillars. Start and sell multiple businesses. Risk: May not stick with one long enough.

### The Builder
Strong Earth element + Direct Wealth. Build sustainable, long-term businesses. Risk: May miss rapid-growth opportunities.

### The Visionary
Strong Power + Output stars. See the future clearly. Risk: May struggle with day-to-day operations.

### The Turnaround Artist
Strong Indirect Power + Resource stars. Thrive in challenges. Risk: May create unnecessary crises.
