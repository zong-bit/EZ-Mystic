# Bazi for Career: How Your Four Pillars Reveal Your Ideal Career Path

Are you in the right career? It's one of the most common questions people bring to a Bazi reading — and one of the areas where Bazi excels.

Unlike personality tests that ask you questions about your preferences, Bazi looks at your **innate energy structure** — the elements you were born with — and maps them to career environments where you'll naturally thrive.

## The 10 Gods (Shi Shen) in Your Career

Your Bazi chart contains **10 Gods** (十神) — relationships between your Day Master and the other Heavenly Stems. Two of them are especially important for career:

### The Wealth Stars (正财/偏财)
These show how you make money:
- **Direct Wealth (正财)**: Stable income, salary jobs, traditional careers
- **Indirect Wealth (偏财)**: Entrepreneurial income, investments, side hustles

**Strong Wealth Star**: You have natural money-making ability
**Weak Wealth Star**: You need to develop financial skills consciously

### The Power Stars (正官/偏官)
These show your authority and career advancement:
- **Direct Power (正官)**: Official positions, management, structured advancement
- **Indirect Power (偏官)**: Entrepreneurship, unconventional careers, competitive fields

**Strong Power Star**: You're naturally suited for leadership
**Weak Power Star**: You may prefer collaborative or support roles

## Your Career Timing

One of Bazi's unique advantages is **career timing**. Your 10-Year Fortune Cycles show when to push forward and when to consolidate:

**Good career years** (your favorable element is strong):
- Start new ventures
- Seek promotions
- Change jobs
- Take calculated risks

**Bad career years** (your unfavorable element is strong):
- Consolidate your position
- Focus on skill development
- Avoid major changes
- Build relationships

## Career by Element Balance

Your overall element balance reveals your career style:

**Strong Day Master + Wealth Star**: Natural entrepreneur. You have the energy and the money-making ability. Take calculated risks.

**Strong Day Master + Power Star**: Natural executive. You're built for leadership. Seek management roles.

**Weak Day Master + Wealth Star**: You need partners or employees to execute your ideas. Build a team.

**Weak Day Master + Power Star**: You respond well to good leadership. Find a mentor or manager who supports your growth.

## Are You in a Career That Fits Your Bazi?

Signs your career **matches** your Bazi:
- Work feels natural, not forced
- You're recognized for your strengths
- You have energy at the end of the day
- Career advancement comes steadily

Signs your career **mismatches** your Bazi:
- Work drains you completely
- You feel undervalued
- You keep hitting the same walls
- Career progress feels blocked
