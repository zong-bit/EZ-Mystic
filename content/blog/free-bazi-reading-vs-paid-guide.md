## The Free Bazi Reading Trap

Most free Bazi reading websites share a common flaw: **they calculate but don't interpret.**

### The "One-Size-Fits-All" Problem

You're given a chart with four pillars, Heavenly Stems, and Earthly Branches. But the tool often produces **generic descriptions** like "You are a Yang Wood Day Master, so you are a leader."

In reality, whether your Day Master is strong or weak changes everything. A weak Yang Wood may be a frustrated leader, while a strong one thrives. Free tools rarely tell you which you are.

### No Context: The Danger of Raw Results

Many free Chinese astrology reports dump raw element counts ("Fire: 3, Wood: 1") without explaining what they mean. They leave you guessing which elements help or hurt you.

### Missing the 10 Gods

Most free calculators omit the **10 Gods system** entirely. Yet the Direct Officer, Indirect Wealth, and Output Stars are the tools that unlock career, relationship, and wealth insights.
