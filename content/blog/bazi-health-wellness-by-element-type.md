# Bazi Health & Wellness Guide: Preventive Care by Five Elements

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), your body is a microcosm of the Five Elements. Your Bazi chart reveals which elements are naturally strong or weak — and therefore which organ systems need extra attention.

This isn't medical advice — it's preventive awareness. Know your constitutional tendencies and you can head off problems before they start.

## Your Constitutional Type

### Wood Type 🌳

**Constitution**: Athletic, flexible, prone to tension
**Weak points**: Liver, eyes, tendons, nervous system
**Common issues**: Eye strain, headaches, PMS, tendonitis, anger issues
**Diet**: Eat leafy greens, avoid alcohol excess, add sour foods
**Exercise**: Stretching, hiking, yoga — avoid overtraining
**Best season**: Spring is peak energy; autumn is vulnerable

### Fire Type 🔥

**Constitution**: Warm, energetic, prone to overheating
**Weak points**: Heart, blood vessels, tongue
**Common issues**: Insomnia, palpitations, high blood pressure, anxiety
**Diet**: Eat cooling foods (bitter melon, cucumber), avoid spicy and deep-fried
**Exercise**: Cardio in moderation, swimming, dancing
**Best season**: Summer is peak; winter is vulnerable

### Earth Type 🏔️

**Constitution**: Solid, stable, prone to stagnation
**Weak points**: Spleen, stomach, digestion, muscles
**Common issues**: Bloating, weight gain, blood sugar issues, worry
**Diet**: Warm, cooked foods; root vegetables; avoid cold/raw
**Exercise**: Walking, gentle yoga, grounding practices
**Best season**: Late summer is peak; spring is vulnerable

### Metal Type ⚔️

**Constitution**: Strong structure, prone to dryness
**Weak points**: Lungs, large intestine, skin, immunity
**Common issues**: Allergies, asthma, dry skin, frequent colds, grief
**Diet**: Moistening foods (pear, honey, radish), avoid dairy excess
**Exercise**: Breathing exercises, swimming, moderate cardio
**Best season**: Autumn is peak; summer is vulnerable

### Water Type 💧

**Constitution**: Deep energy, prone to cold
**Weak points**: Kidneys, bladder, bones, hormones, ears
**Common issues**: Lower back pain, urinary issues, fertility, hearing, fear
**Diet**: Warm, salty foods (seaweed, bone broth); avoid dehydration
**Exercise**: Gentle movement, qigong, walking — avoid overwork
**Best season**: Winter is peak; late summer is vulnerable

## Seasonal Preventive Care

| Season | Dominant Element | Focus | Practice |
|--------|-----------------|-------|----------|
| Spring | Wood | Liver detox | Light diet, early rising |
| Summer | Fire | Heart health | Rest at midday, stay cool |
| Late Summer | Earth | Digestion | Warm meals, regular schedule |
| Autumn | Metal | Lung health | Moisturize, breathe deeply |
| Winter | Water | Kidney rest | Sleep more, conserve energy |

## Daily Practices by Element

**Start your morning based on your missing element**:
- Missing Wood: Morning stretch + green smoothie
- Missing Fire: Morning sun exposure + journaling
- Missing Earth: Grounding walk + warm breakfast
- Missing Metal: Deep breathing + dry brushing
- Missing Water: Hydrate + gentle movement

## The Mind-Body Connection

Your Bazi chart doesn't just show physical tendencies — it shows emotional ones too:

- **Excess Wood**: Prone to anger. Practice forgiveness.
- **Excess Fire**: Prone to anxiety. Practice stillness.
- **Excess Earth**: Prone to worry. Practice letting go.
- **Excess Metal**: Prone to grief. Practice openness.
- **Excess Water**: Prone to fear. Practice courage.

Emotional health IS physical health in Chinese medicine.
