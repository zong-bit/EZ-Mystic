## Are You Built for Business or Employment?

The Ten Gods in your chart reveal your innate work style. Here's the quick guide:

### Entrepreneurial Patterns (十神)

| Pattern | Entrepreneurial Potential | Best Business Type |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Strong Seven Killings (七杀)** | Very high | Competitive, high-risk ventures; startups; leadership roles |
| **Strong Indirect Wealth (偏财)** | Very high | Sales-based businesses; investing; deal-making; networking |
| **Strong Hurting Officer (伤官)** | High | Creative businesses; innovation; technology; consulting |
| **Strong Direct Output (食神)** | Moderate-High | Creative businesses; food; arts; education; content |
| **Strong Indirect Companion (劫财)** | Moderate | Team-based businesses; community-driven ventures; social enterprise |

### Employment-Friendly Patterns

| Pattern | Best Work Environment |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Strong Direct Officer (正官)** | Large organizations, government, structured corporations |
| **Strong Direct Resource (正印)** | Academia, research, publishing, traditional institutions |
| **Strong Direct Wealth (正财)** | Stable salaried positions, consistent income environments |
| **Strong Direct Companion (比肩)** | Independent work, solo practice, freelance |

### The Hybrid Pattern

Many people have both entrepreneurial and employment patterns in their chart. For example:
- **Strong Direct Officer + Strong Indirect Wealth:** You thrive in corporate leadership but have entrepreneurial side-hustle potential
- **Strong Direct Resource + Strong Hurting Officer:** You're a scholar-entrepreneur — someone who builds businesses around knowledge and expertise

## Your Wealth Pattern: How You Make Money

Your Bazi chart reveals your **wealth capacity** and **wealth pattern** — two different things.

### Wealth Capacity (命局财星)

This is your innate financial potential, determined by the Wealth Stars in your birth chart:

- **Strong Wealth Stars + Strong Day Master:** High wealth capacity — you can accumulate significant wealth
- **Strong Wealth Stars + Weak Day Master:** Wealth opportunities exist, but you need to strengthen yourself first
- **Weak Wealth Stars + Strong Day Master:** You have the capacity to earn, but need to create the opportunities
- **Weak Wealth Stars + Weak Day Master:** Wealth comes through indirect means (career, skills, network) rather than direct business ventures

### Wealth Pattern (财星类型)

Your Wealth Stars also reveal *how* you'll make money:

| Pattern | Wealth Source | Timeline |
|---------|------------|----------|
| **Direct Wealth dominant** | Salary, steady business revenue | Gradual, predictable |
| **Indirect Wealth dominant** | Investments, windfalls, side income | Variable, opportunistic |
| **Output → Wealth** | Skills, creativity, innovation | Builds over time |
| **Officer → Wealth** | Position, authority, career | Steady career progression |
| **Wealth Storage (财库)** | Accumulated wealth, inheritance | Long-term, quiet |

## Timing Your Business Launch

This is where Bazi gets truly practical. Your chart reveals the *best* and *worst* timing for business ventures.

### Favorable Timing Indicators

A year is favorable for launching a business when:
1. **Your Wealth Star is activated** — the year's element produces or activates your Wealth Star
2. **Your Favorable Element is strong** — the year supports your chart's balance (see our [favorable element guide](/blog/bazi-strong-weak-day-master-favorable-element-guide))
3. **Your Luck Pillar supports business** — your current 10-year period activates entrepreneurial stars

### Unfavorable Timing Indicators

A year is challenging for business launches when:
1. **Your Unfavorable Element is strong** — the year destabilizes your chart
2. **Your Spouse Star is activated** (for women) or **Wife Star** (for men) — relationship priorities may compete with business focus
3. **Your Luck Pillar is in a "consolidation" phase** — better to strengthen foundations than expand
