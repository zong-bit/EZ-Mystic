## FAQ: Ox Zodiac 2026

### Q: Is the Ox-Horse relationship really that neutral?
It's not truly "neutral" — it's **productive but paced differently**. The Fire→Earth cycle is favorable, but the Horse year moves faster than your natural rhythm. Think of it as receiving support that arrives at a pace you need to adjust to, rather than resistance.

### Q: What if I'm in the Goat-Ox clash month this year?
The Goat-Ox clash (丑未冲) occurs during the **Goat month**, typically in September. During this period, be extra cautious about:
- Signing major contracts or agreements
- Making large financial investments
- Starting new business ventures

These are temporary — the clash energy concentrates in that month and then releases.

### Q: Can I use Feng Shui to improve my 2026 fortune?
Yes. Placing **Fire element items** (red objects, candles, warm lighting) in the South area of your home transforms the year's energy positively. Wearing Fire-color clothing (red, purple) on important days also helps.

### Q: When exactly does the Ox-Horse year begin and end?
The Chinese zodiac year begins at **Lichun (立春)**, approximately February 4, 2026. It ends on February 3, 2027. However, the energy transition begins about two weeks before Lichun (mid-January).

### Q: What about my children who are also Oxen?
Children's zodiac signs matter, but their **Bazi charts** (especially Day Master strength) are far more important. A child Ox with strong support in their full chart will handle 2026's pace challenges much better than one without. Focus on maintaining routine and providing emotional support during this period.
